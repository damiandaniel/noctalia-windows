# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a friendly place to learn and build things. This applies to everyone, in issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other space for this project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here have different skills and different amounts of time.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Argue about the idea, not the person.
- Say it clearly and give reasons. Don't assume the worst about someone's intent.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Say thanks when someone helps.
- Own your mistakes, and fix them.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, name-calling, or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Hateful or demeaning comments about who someone is, including their race, gender, age,
  disability, religion, sexuality, nationality, or experience level.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention.
- Threats, or sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after the answer is clear.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

The maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and commits that break these
rules. They can also warn someone, or block them from the project for a while or for good.

We will usually start with a friendly note. For serious or repeated problems, we may skip that step.

## How to report a problem

Email **damiand@fedoraproject.org**. Please include what happened, where, and links if you have them.

Reports are private. We will look into each one fairly and will not share who sent it, except
where the law requires it. If the report is about a maintainer, we will ask someone else to
handle it where we can.

## If this project moves

If this repository moves to the [noctalia-dev](https://github.com/noctalia-dev) organization, the
organization's own rules and contacts will apply from then on.
